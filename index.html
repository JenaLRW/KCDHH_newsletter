Protect Your Hearing Month 2026

October is Protect Your Hearing Month. The observance raises awareness about noise-induced hearing loss (NIHL). This type of hearing loss happens when loud sounds damage the tiny hair cells in the inner ear. These hair cells play an important role in hearing by converting sound vibrations into electrical signals that travel to the brain, where they are interpreted as sound. Because the body cannot replace damaged hair cells, the resulting hearing loss is usually permanent.

Hearing protection is an important part of hearing health for everyone, including people who are already deaf or hard of hearing. Visit the CDCs LISTEN UP! PROTECT YOUR HEARING page to learn more.

Document Submission Guidelines

KCDHH accepts submissions relevant to the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community, their families, service providers, and community partners. This includes events, announcements, and resources.

Submissions should include:

• Title of event or announcement

• Date and time

• Location (in-person or virtual)

• Contact information for person who can answer questions related to the information

• Brief description (100 words or fewer)

• Must state whether or not accommodations will be provided and if not, the name and contact information for the person that needs to be contacted to request it.

Information must be submitted in PDF format attached to an email.

All submitted documents must meet federal accessibility standards. KCDHH does not edit documents for accessibility. If your document is not accessible, please provide a direct link to a webpage or social media page where the information can be found and we will share the link.

KCDHH reserves the right to edit, accept, or decline any submission.
